Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Social Media Publishing App handles information when connecting to authorised social media accounts.

1. Purpose of the application

The application is a private content-management and publishing tool used by its owner to upload and publish authorised video content to social media accounts controlled by the owner.

2. Information accessed

Depending on the connected platform and permissions granted, the application may access:

  • Basic account or Page information
  • Account identifiers
  • Publishing permissions
  • Access tokens required for authorised API requests
  • Post, video, and performance information made available by the platform

3. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorised accounts, upload and publish content, retrieve permitted performance data, and operate the owner's private automation workflows.

4. Data sharing

The application does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

5. Data storage and security

Access tokens and credentials are stored in secured automation services and are used only for authorised publishing and account-management actions.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and account data from unauthorised access.

6. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the application, maintain authorised connections, troubleshoot errors, and comply with applicable legal requirements.
